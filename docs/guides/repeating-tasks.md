# Repeating tasks

Some things come round again and again. The gym. The rent. Watering the plants. Brushing our teeth before bed (hopefully). In Vellum these are repeating tasks: tick one and it comes straight back on its next day. It's the same task every time, not a pile of copies. It just moves forward, and the days we've done it collect in a Done list at the bottom of its page.

That part is easy. The tricky part is life. We forget to tick it. We skip a day. We go to the gym on Saturday instead of Friday. We're away for a long weekend and the plants ... well, someone watered them, right? Hopefully.

So here's what's in store. First the basics: setting a repeat, moving it just this once without breaking its rhythm, and the three ways to pause or stop one. Then a set of examples, one per situation, each with what to click and what you'll see: a late week at the gym, a missed night of teeth brushing, the plants after a long weekend away, a bill paid late, a water filter that counts from the day it's changed, a day we recorded wrong, and a tick we didn't mean. Maybe you relate to one or more of these. Gym turns up in a few, because Gym is where most of our weeks go sideways. And at the end, a few handy extras: notes on a done day, and using the keyboard.

Every picture on this page was taken on Monday, October 5, so in the examples "today" is that Monday.

NB: `Cmd` throughout is the Mac key. Use `Ctrl` on Windows and Linux.

## Setting a repeat

Let's start with a task that repeats for a while and then stops. Say you're on antibiotics for five days.

On today's page, click `+ add item`, type `Take antibiotics #task` and hit `Enter` to take **#task** from the list. Click the bullet dot next to it to open it on its own page.

Click the When box and type `every day, 5 times`. Before you hit `Enter`, the line under the box reads back what it understood.

![The When box with every day, 5 times typed in, and the line under it reading When Monday, October 5, then every day, 5 times](/images/repeating-tasks/antibiotics-typed.webp)

Hit `Enter`. The When box now holds today's date, with the repeat in grey beside it.

![Take antibiotics with When October 5, 2026 and every day, 5 times beside it](/images/repeating-tasks/antibiotics-set.webp)

Tick it and it comes straight back tomorrow. The message at the bottom of the window says so: **Take antibiotics done. Back on Tuesday, October 6**. On the fifth tick the message is **Take antibiotics done for the last time. The repeat is finished**, and the task stays done.

### Other ways to say it

The When box understands most of the ways you'd say a repeat out loud. A few to try, with what the line under the box reads back:

| Type this | The line under the box reads |
| --- | --- |
| `every mon, wed and fri` | When Monday, October 5, then every Monday, Wednesday and Friday |
| `every month on the 1st` | When Sunday, November 1, then every month on the 1st |
| `7 days after done` | When Monday, October 12, then 7 days after each time it is done |
| `every day until friday` | When Monday, October 5, then every day until October 9 |
| `every monday, 4 times` | When Monday, October 5, then every Monday, 4 times |

That `7 days after done` one is a different animal. It doesn't follow the calendar, it counts from the day you tick it. We'll meet it again with the water filter.

### Or pick it from a menu

If you'd rather click than type, click the small calendar at the right end of the When box. Near the bottom, **Repeats** opens a menu built from the task's date.

![The Repeats menu: Does not repeat, Every day, Every weekday, Every Monday and more, then a line pointing at the When box](/images/repeating-tasks/repeats-menu.webp)

Pick one, and **Ends** appears under it, saying when the repeat stops: **Never**, **On a day…** or **After a number of times…**.

![The Ends menu: Never, On a day, After a number of times](/images/repeating-tasks/ends-menu.webp)

And once a repeat is set, **Pause** and **Stop repeating** sit underneath those two, for when the series takes a break or is over for good. More on those two in a moment.

## Moving it just this once

Physio is every Wednesday. This week the physio can only do Thursday. We don't want to change the whole series, just this one session.

Open Physio and click in its When box, which reads October 7, 2026. Delete what's there, type `thursday` and hit `Enter`.

![Physio's When box reading October 8, 2026, and under it: This time only. Every week it is Wednesday. Use Thursday from now on](/images/repeating-tasks/physio-once.webp)

The When box now reads October 8, 2026, that Thursday, and the line under it says what that means: **This time only. Every week it is Wednesday.** Once we tick Thursday's session, Physio goes straight back to its usual day: **Physio done. Back on Wednesday, October 14**.

And if the physio has moved to Thursdays for good? Click **Use Thursday from now on** instead. **Physio. From now on When is every Thursday**.

## Pausing, stopping, or canceling for good

There are three ways to take a repeat out of our week, and each does something different.

### Pause: a break, then back to normal

Piano lessons are every Tuesday, but the teacher is away for a month. Open Piano lessons, click the small calendar beside its When box, and click **Pause** at the bottom.

![Piano lessons with an empty When box and the word paused beside it](/images/repeating-tasks/piano-paused.webp)

The When box empties, and beside it, where every Tuesday was, it now says **paused**. When lessons start again, open the same calendar and click **Resume**. Piano lessons comes back on its next Tuesday, and its Done list is just as it was.

### Stop repeating: one last time

Swimming lessons are every Thursday, and the course ends this week. Open Swimming lessons, click the small calendar beside its When box, and click **Stop repeating**.

![Swimming lessons with When October 8, 2026 and no repeat beside it](/images/repeating-tasks/swim-stopped.webp)

The repeat comes off, and the When box keeps this Thursday's date. So the last lesson is still there as an ordinary task, and once it's ticked, that's the end of it. The Done list keeps every lesson so far.

### Cancel the series: done with it for good

Book club meets every other Thursday, and the club has folded 😞. Open Book club and `Shift`-click the checkbox beside its title.

![The menu on Book club's checkbox: Complete, Cancel and Cancel the series](/images/repeating-tasks/book-menu.webp)

Click **Cancel the series**. **Book club canceled. The repeat is stopped**. The task itself is canceled, the repeat comes off, and the Done list gets one last line: today's date, struck through, with **for October 8, 2026** and **canceled** beside it.

That menu also shows two keys: `Cmd-Enter` ticks and `Shift-Cmd-Enter` cancels. More on those at the end.

## Gym, a week gone sideways

Gym repeats every Monday, Wednesday and Friday. We last ticked it on Monday, September 28. Today is Monday, October 5, and Gym's When is still Wednesday, September 30. That makes it late: a repeating task is late once the day it's waiting on has passed.

What really happened that week? We skipped Wednesday. And we went on Saturday instead of Friday. And we're going again tonight, honest.

One idea before we start, because everything on this page rests on it. **Each choice records the one day you pick.** If that's the day the task is waiting on, or later, the task moves on past it. Anything earlier that you didn't record stays blank. Blank is fine. It just means nobody wrote anything down.

### The menu on the checkbox

Click the bullet dot next to Gym to open it on its own page, then `Shift`-click the checkbox beside its title (right-click works as well).

![The menu on Gym's checkbox: Done today, Done Wednesday, September 30, Canceled today, Canceled Wednesday, September 30, Pick a day, and Cancel the series](/images/repeating-tasks/gym-menu.webp)

The menu names the day Gym is waiting on, so we don't have to remember it. **Canceled today** is there because today is a Monday, one of Gym's own days. On a task whose day isn't today, rent on the 1st say, it won't show.

That menu only shows up once a task is late. On a task that's on time, the checkbox menu is just **Complete**, **Cancel** and **Cancel the series**. To fill in a past day on it, open the task and use **Add a day** beside **DONE**. That heading only appears once the task has been ticked at least once.

### Skipping Wednesday

Click **Canceled Wednesday, September 30**. The message at the bottom of the window says **Gym canceled. Still late: waiting on Friday, October 2**. Friday has gone by as well, so Gym is still late, now waiting on Friday.

### Saturday, for Friday

`Shift`-click the checkbox again and this time click **Pick a day…**. The menu turns into a month, with **Done** selected at the top.

![The Pick a day month: Done selected, October 2 ringed, today filled in, September 28 and 30 faint with a dot under each](/images/repeating-tasks/gym-pick.webp)

The ring is the day Gym is waiting on, and the filled circle is today. Days after today are faint, because nobody has been to the gym tomorrow. September 28 and 30 are faint with a dot under them: they're already on Gym's list, so they can't be picked twice.

Leave **Done** as it is and click `3`.

![The message: Gym done Saturday, October 3. Back today](/images/repeating-tasks/gym-toast.webp)

Saturday isn't a Gym day, so Vellum counts it as making up the most recent session before it, which is Friday's. Then Gym moves on to its next day after Saturday. That's today, so it's back on today's list and no longer late.

### Tonight, honest

We're definitely going tonight, so let's tick Gym's checkbox now, the normal way, and save ourselves the trouble later. **Gym done. Back on Wednesday, October 7**. Hold that thought.

### What Gym's Done list says

We're still on Gym's page, so just scroll down to **DONE**.

![Gym's Done list: October 5, then October 3 for October 2, then September 30 struck through and canceled, then the ticks before](/images/repeating-tasks/gym-done.webp)

The whole week, in order. Saturday's line says **for October 2, 2026**, meaning done on Saturday for Friday's session. Wednesday is struck through with **canceled** beside it. Nothing is blank this time, because we settled every day. We didn't have to.

## Brush teeth, and last night

Brush teeth PM repeats every day. Today is Monday, and it's still waiting on yesterday, Sunday. We forgot to tick it last night.

Open Brush teeth PM (its bullet dot) and `Shift`-click the checkbox beside its title.

![The menu on Brush teeth PM: Done today, Done yesterday, Canceled today, Canceled yesterday, Pick a day, and Cancel the series](/images/repeating-tasks/brush-menu.webp)

When the day it's waiting on was yesterday, the menu just says yesterday.

- We did brush and just forgot to tick it? Click **Done yesterday**. The message says **Brush teeth PM done. Back today**. Sunday is recorded, and it stays on today's list for tonight.
- We skipped it (no judgement)? Click **Canceled yesterday**. **Brush teeth PM canceled. Back today**. Same place, a different line on its list.

Clicked the wrong one? `Cmd-Z` puts the task, its date and its Done list back exactly as they were. That goes for every choice on this page.

## Water plants, and a long weekend away

Water plants repeats every day. We watered on Wednesday, September 30, then went away Thursday to Sunday. Today is Monday and it's waiting on Thursday, October 1.

### If the skips don't matter

Water them, then tick the checkbox the normal way. **Water plants done. Back on Tuesday, October 6**. Monday is recorded, Thursday to Sunday stay blank, and that's the end of it. Most of the time, that's all it needs.

### If you want the skips on record

Maybe someone else was watering them and we'd like to know how often the plants went without. Click the bullet dot next to Water plants to open it, and scroll down to **DONE**. Beside the count is **Add a day**. Click it, switch the top of the month to **Canceled**, and click `3`.

![The Add a day month under Water plants' Done heading, Canceled selected, the 1st to the 4th dark, September 28 to 30 faint with a dot](/images/repeating-tasks/plants-add-a-day.webp)

The ring around today is just where the month opens. It doesn't mean anything here.

**Water plants canceled Saturday, October 3. Back on Tuesday, October 6**. Now do the same for the 1st, the 2nd and the 4th: click **Add a day**, switch to **Canceled** (the month opens with **Done** selected every time), and click the day. Any order works. The list sorts itself by day.

![Water plants' Done list: October 5, then October 4, 3, 2 and 1 struck through and canceled, then the waterings before the trip](/images/repeating-tasks/plants-done.webp)

None of this moves Water plants. It stays on Tuesday, where today's tick put it.

## Pay rent, paid on the 3rd

Pay rent repeats on the 1st of every month. We paid it on Saturday the 3rd and never ticked it, so on Monday it's still waiting on Thursday, October 1. Open Pay rent and `Shift`-click the checkbox beside its title.

![The menu on Pay rent: Done today, Done Thursday, October 1, Canceled Thursday, October 1, Pick a day, and Cancel the series](/images/repeating-tasks/rent-menu.webp)

No **Canceled today** this time. Today isn't the 1st, so there's no rent to cancel today.

Click **Pick a day…**, leave **Done** selected, and click `3`. **Pay rent done Saturday, October 3. Back on Sunday, November 1**.

![Pay rent's Done list: October 3 for October 1, then September 1](/images/repeating-tasks/rent-done.webp)

The 3rd isn't a rent day, so, like Gym's Saturday, it makes up the most recent session before it: **October 3, 2026** **for October 1, 2026**. And the rent is back on November 1, not November 3. Paying late doesn't move next month's rent.

## Change water filter, 7 days after it's done

This one counts from the day it's done: each time we tick it, the next one is 7 days later. We last changed it on Saturday, September 26, so its When is Saturday, October 3, and today it's late. Open Change water filter and `Shift`-click the checkbox beside its title.

![The menu on Change water filter: Done today, Done Saturday, October 3, Canceled Saturday, October 3, Pick a day, and Cancel the series](/images/repeating-tasks/filter-menu.webp)

No **Canceled today** here either. A task counted from when it's done only has the one day it's waiting on, so there's nothing to cancel today.

Three ways the weekend could have gone. Pick the one that happened.

- **We changed it on Sunday and forgot to tick it.** **Pick a day…**, leave **Done** selected, click `4`. **Change water filter done yesterday. Back on Sunday, October 11**. Seven days after Sunday.
- **We're changing it right now.** Tick the checkbox. **Change water filter done. Back on Monday, October 12**. Seven days after today.
- **We're skipping this one.** Click **Canceled Saturday, October 3**. **Change water filter canceled. Back on Saturday, October 10**. Seven days after the Saturday it was due.

Same filter, three different next dates. The next one always counts from the day we pick.

## Fixing a day we got wrong

Everything so far settles a day that hasn't been recorded yet. But sometimes the day we recorded is the wrong one.

### Brush teeth, ticked this morning for last night

We brushed last night, forgot to tick it, and ticked Brush teeth PM first thing this morning instead. Vellum heard "done today": **Brush teeth PM done. Back on Tuesday, October 6**. So it's gone from today's list, and tonight's brush has nowhere to go.

Click the bullet dot next to Brush teeth PM to open it, and scroll down to **DONE**. Point at the top line, **October 5, 2026**, and three dots appear just after its time, with a `+` beside them. Click the dots, not the `+` (right-clicking the line opens the same menu).

![The menu on a Done line: Change the day and Remove this line](/images/repeating-tasks/brush-line-menu.webp)

Click **Change the day…**. The month opens on the line's own day, with **Done** selected.

![The Change the day month: Done selected, October 5 ringed, the 1st to the 3rd faint with a dot](/images/repeating-tasks/brush-change.webp)

Click `4`. **Brush teeth PM done yesterday. Back today**.

![Brush teeth PM's Done list, now October 4, 3, 2 and 1](/images/repeating-tasks/brush-done.webp)

The line now reads October 4, and Brush teeth is back on today's list where it belongs. A repeating task follows its Done list: fix the list and the task moves to match, as long as we haven't typed a new date into its When box since. A date we typed ourselves stays where we put it.

### Gym, and the Wednesday we didn't skip

Thinking about it, we did go on Wednesday. Late, after work, but we went. Open Gym, point at the struck-through **September 30, 2026** line, click the three dots and choose **Change the day…**. This time the month opens with **Canceled** selected, because that's what the line says. Click **Done** at the top, then `30`.

**Gym done Wednesday, September 30. Back on Wednesday, October 7**.

![Gym's Done list with September 30 now done, no longer struck through](/images/repeating-tasks/gym-done-fixed.webp)

The **Done** and **Canceled** switch at the top of the month is how a line changes from one to the other. And because this was an older line, Gym doesn't move. It's still on Wednesday.

## Ticked by mistake

Remember how we were definitely going to the gym tonight? We ticked it at 10 this morning to save ourselves the trouble later. And then it rained. (I know, terrible excuse, but hey, this is only a demo, right? 😁)

Open Gym, point at today's line, **October 5, 2026**, click the three dots and choose **Remove this line**.

![The message: Line removed. Gym is back today](/images/repeating-tasks/gym-removed.webp)

The line is gone and Gym is back on today's list, waiting for a night when it doesn't rain. Removing the line that moved a repeating task on puts the task back where it was.

## Handy extras

### A note on a done day

Each line in a repeating task's Done list can hold notes of its own. Open Gym, point at the **September 28, 2026** line, and click the `+` at its right end, just after the three dots. Type `Legs day, then a 5k run` and hit `Escape`.

![Gym's Done list with Legs day, then a 5k run as a note under September 28, 2026](/images/repeating-tasks/gym-note.webp)

The note sits right under its day.

### The keyboard

With the cursor in a task's title, `Cmd-Enter` ticks it and `Shift-Cmd-Enter` cancels it. On a late task they do what the menu does.

- `Cmd-Enter` is **Done today**. Say Morning walk repeats every day and is still waiting on Saturday: **Morning walk done. Back on Tuesday, October 6**.
- `Shift-Cmd-Enter` on a late task cancels today if today is one of the task's days, and otherwise the most recent of its days before today. (On a task that isn't late, it cancels the session in its When box.) Floss, every day, still waiting on Saturday: today is canceled, and **Floss canceled. Back on Tuesday, October 6**. Bins out, every Thursday, still waiting on October 1: that Thursday is canceled, and **Bins out canceled. Back on Thursday, October 8**.

## Wrapping up

So ... there we have it. A dozen or so tasks, one menu on the checkbox, one on each Done line, and a calendar that pops up whenever a day needs picking.

The ideas underneath it all:

- **A date you type is for this time only.** The repeat keeps its rhythm. When the repeat is on one day, like every Wednesday or the 1st of the month, the line under the box also offers **Use … from now on**.
- **Pause, Stop repeating and Cancel the series** are three different stops: a break, a last time, and done for good.
- **Each choice records the one day you pick.** Settling the day a task is waiting on moves it on past that day. Filling in an older day doesn't move it. Anything you don't record stays blank, and blank is fine.
- **A day that isn't one of the task's own days makes up the most recent session before it.** Saturday for Friday's gym, the 3rd for the 1st's rent.
- **The checkbox menu settles a late task.** `Shift`-click or right-click the checkbox, on the task's own page or in Today's tasks.
- **The Done list fixes, fills in or removes a day**, in any order. Done can go on any past day, canceled only on one of the task's own days.
- **A repeating task follows its Done list.** Fix the list and the task moves to match, unless you've typed a new date into its When box since.
- **`Cmd-Z` takes any of it back**, in one step.

Nothing here is special to gyms and houseplants. Swap them for piano practice, the electric bill or the dog's flea treatment, and the same few moves cover every week that doesn't go to plan. Which, let's be honest, is most of them 😁
